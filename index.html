Academic and Theological Integrity

Our Verified Sources

Every answer, article, and explanation on this platform is grounded in reputable, peer-reviewed Biblical scholarship and verified historical research.

Primary Text
King James Bible (1611)
The authorized translation commissioned by King James I, drawn from the Masoretic Text (Hebrew) and Textus Receptus (Greek). The most widely read English translation in history.
Commentary
Matthew Henry's Commentary
Completed 1714 — six volumes covering every chapter of Scripture, one of the most thorough devotional commentaries ever written.
Reference
Easton's Bible Dictionary
M.G. Easton's scholarly 1897 dictionary provides historical, geographical, and theological context for over 4,000 Biblical terms and proper names.
Archaeology
Archaeological Study Bible
Published with contributions from the American Schools of Oriental Research — 3,500 archaeological notes connected to specific Biblical texts and events.
Apologetics
Answers in Genesis
World-leading creation and apologetics ministry providing research and resources that defend the historical accuracy and authority of the Bible from Genesis to Revelation.
Study Tool
Blue Letter Bible
Comprehensive online resource with access to original Hebrew and Greek texts, lexicons, concordances, and scholarly commentaries aligned verse-by-verse.
Theology
Spurgeon's Treasury of David
Charles Haddon Spurgeon's seven-volume commentary on the Psalms — a masterwork of devotional exposition by the Prince of Preachers.
Historical
Josephus — Antiquities and Wars
First-century Jewish historian Flavius Josephus provides crucial non-Biblical corroboration of Old and New Testament people, places, and events.
Manuscripts
Dead Sea Scrolls
Discovered 1947-1956 at Qumran — confirmed remarkable accuracy of Old Testament transmission over 1,000 years. The greatest manuscript discovery in history.

Why We Built This

The Bible is the most studied, most translated, most verified, and most transformative document in human history — and yet Biblical literacy is declining in the very culture that was shaped by it. People are not rejecting Scripture because they have examined it and found it wanting. They are drifting from it because no one has made it accessible enough, credible enough, or relevant enough to hold their attention.

The Living Word was built to change that. Using AI grounded in centuries of verified Biblical scholarship, we give every person — regardless of church background, education level, or prior Bible knowledge — the tools to encounter Scripture on its own terms and discover why it has spoken to every generation for three thousand years.

We are not building a church. We are building a door. What happens when people walk through it is between them and God.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

The Living Word is built on the historic, orthodox Christian faith as revealed in the King James Bible. The following statements summarize what we believe and what guides every feature of this platform.

① The Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God. Every word of the original manuscripts was given by God through human authors, making Scripture completely truthful and trustworthy in all that it affirms.

"All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness." — 2 Timothy 3:16
② God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — co-equal, co-eternal, and co-substantial. God is the Creator of all things, sovereign over all things, and perfectly holy, just, and loving in His nature.

"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD." — Deuteronomy 6:4
③ Jesus Christ

We believe in the Lord Jesus Christ — fully God and fully man — born of a virgin, sinless in life, crucified for our sins, bodily risen from the dead on the third day, ascended to the Father, and coming again in glory. He is the only mediator between God and man, and salvation is found in no one else.

"I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me." — John 14:6
④ The Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit, the third person of the Trinity, who convicts the world of sin, regenerates believers at salvation, indwells every Christian, and produces spiritual fruit. He inspired the Scriptures and illumines them to the hearts of those who seek understanding.

"For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind." — 2 Timothy 1:7
⑤ Mankind and Sin

We believe that God created humanity in His image and likeness, with inherent dignity and eternal worth. Through Adam's fall, all humanity inherited a sinful nature and stands in need of redemption. No person is able to save themselves by their own goodness or effort.

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God." — Romans 3:23
⑥ Salvation

We believe that salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. It is the free gift of God — not earned by works, merit, or religious observance. All who repent of their sin and place their trust in Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour are forgiven, justified, and given eternal life.

"For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God." — Ephesians 2:8
⑦ The Church

We believe in the universal church — the body of all true believers — and in the importance of local church community, fellowship, accountability, and worship. The Living Word is not a substitute for the local church; it is a resource to complement and enrich it.

"Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together." — Hebrews 10:25
⑧ Creation

We believe God created the heavens and the earth and all living things. We affirm the historical reality of the accounts of creation and the fall of mankind as recorded in Genesis. We hold that Scripture and genuine science, rightly understood, are fully compatible, and we encourage honest inquiry into both.

"In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth." — Genesis 1:1
⑨ Eternity

We believe in the bodily resurrection of the dead, the final judgment, and the eternal existence of all people — believers to eternal life with God, and unbelievers to eternal separation from Him. We hold these truths with both conviction and compassion, and they are the reason we pursue every person with the Gospel.

"And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death." — Revelation 21:4
